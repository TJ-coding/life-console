01 STRATEGISE

“My long-term goals fizzle out.”

Break a vision into goals and milestones.

Tag a task @SIG-01.2 and it counts toward that milestone.

02 DISTRIBUTE

“My week is full before it starts.”

Spread tasks over the days that have room.

Each day has a 7-hour budget. Auto time-box fits tasks into free gaps by priority.

03 EXECUTE

“What should I be doing right now?”

The current block, counting down.

Open it and the task in front of you is already there.

Start with one goal.
