Life Console vs Sunsama

Checked 10 November 2026 against sunsama.com/pricing and sunsama.com, sunsama.com/a/yc

Both are daily planners built around time-blocking. Sunsama is a paid hosted service that pulls work in from your other tools and calendars. Life Console is free, open source and self-hostable, but has no calendar sync or integrations.

Pick Sunsama if

  • your day lives in Google, Outlook or Apple calendar and you want tasks next to your meetings
  • your tasks live in Asana, Jira, Linear, Notion or Trello and you want them in one daily list
  • you want native desktop apps

Pick Life Console if

  • you don't want a subscription, or want to run your planner on your own server
  • you plan from long-term goals down to today's tasks
  • you want deadlines as time left rather than dates

Open Life Console: free, sign in with Google.
