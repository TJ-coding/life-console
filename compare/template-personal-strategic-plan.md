## How to fill it in

1. **Write the vision in one sentence**, as a state you can recognise: "Working as a data analyst, in a team I like, by June 2027", not "get better at my career". The **intent** says why, so you can tell later whether a shortcut still serves it.
2. **Pick two or three goals, and rank them.** More than three and none get the week. Each one needs **indicators of success**: what you'd see if it worked.
3. **Break each goal into milestones** with **specific, measurable criteria** and a **time bound**: "Finish 4 portfolio projects, each published with a write-up, by 31 March". A milestone you can't measure can't be checked. A goal's % done is the average of its milestones'.
4. **Set a check frequency** (every two weeks works for most plans) and put the first date in your calendar. A plan that's never checked quietly turns into a wish.
5. **Decide in advance** what you'll do if a milestone succeeds or fails. That's the difference between a plan and a hope: you've already chosen the next move.
6. **Link this week's tasks to a milestone.** If nothing on your list serves the plan this week, that's the first thing to fix.

## Example: a career change in 18 months

| Plan | |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Working as a data analyst in a team I like, by June 2027 |
| **Intent** | Work I find interesting, with skills that keep growing |
| **Values** | Keep weekends with family; no debt for courses |
| **Check** | Every 2 weeks, Sunday evening |

| Priority | Goal | Indicators of success | Time bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Job-ready skills | Can do a take-home analysis task in under 4 hours | 31 Dec 2026 |
| 2 | A portfolio that gets interviews | 3 interview invitations from 20 applications | 31 Mar 2027 |
| 3 | Network in the field | 10 conversations with working analysts | Continuous |

| Milestone | Serves | Specific and measurable criteria | Time bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| SQL and Python course | 1 | Course finished, all exercises passed | 15 Oct 2026 |
| Statistics refresh | 1 | 40 practice problems at 80% or better | 30 Nov 2026 |
| Portfolio projects | 2 | 4 projects published, each with a write-up | 31 Mar 2027 |
| Applications | 2 | 20 tailored applications sent | 31 May 2027 |
| Coffee chats | 3 | 1 conversation every 2 weeks | Continuous |

**Decision point:** if fewer than 3 interviews come from the first 20 applications, spend two weeks rewriting the portfolio's write-ups before sending more.

## Do it in Life Console instead (free)

![A strategic plan in Life Console: vision, goals and milestones in a cascading diagram, with progress and time bounds](/media/plan@2x.webp)

Life Console is the free, open-source app this template turned into. The **Strategy** screen holds the same plan: vision and intent, prioritised goals (PSGs) and measurable milestones (SIGs) with time bounds, a check frequency with *Log check today*, and the events timetable. What paper can't do:

- **Tasks link to milestones.** Tag a task with `@SIG-01.2` and it shows up under that milestone, so you see this week's work feeding the plan.
- **Goal flow:** the plan drawn as a timeline of what depends on what, with late milestones in red. Drag a card to move its date.
- **Reminders:** your goals' dates show up next to your deadlines, in Plan week and in the morning briefing, so the plan comes to you.
