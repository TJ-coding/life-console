# Life Console vs Structured

<p class="meta">Features and prices checked 27 November 2026 against <a href="https://help.structured.app/en/articles/1897986" rel="nofollow noopener">Structured's free and Pro feature list</a>, <a href="https://structured.app/" rel="nofollow noopener">structured.app</a> and its <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner/id1499198946" rel="nofollow noopener">US App Store listing</a>. Written by the developer of Life Console.</p>

<p class="summary">Both put your day on one timeline instead of a bare to-do list. <b>Structured</b> is a polished day planner for your phone: a colourful timeline, native apps on every Apple device and Android, and Pro features like calendar import and an AI that builds your day from a sentence. <b>Life Console</b> is free, open source and self-hostable, and plans further out: from long-term goals and deadlines to the week, then today.</p>

| | Life Console | Structured |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free, no paid tier | Free core app with no ads; Pro monthly, yearly or $99.99 once (US App Store), priced by region |
| Source code | Open source, AGPL-3.0 | Closed |
| Self-hosting | Yes, Docker Compose ([guide](/docs/self-hosting)) | No; syncs through Structured Cloud |
| Apps | Web app that installs on phones and desktops and works offline; Mac menu-bar app | Native iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android apps, plus a web app |
| Day view | Day timeline with the current block counting down | One visual timeline of tasks, with an inbox for untimed ones |
| Planning the week | **Plan week**: a 7-hour budget per day, drag from the backlog, **Auto time-box** fills free gaps by priority | Weekly and monthly views of the timeline |
| Calendars | No calendar sync; meetings go in as fixed blocks | Imports Google, Outlook and Apple calendars and Reminders (Pro) |
| Repeating tasks | Weekly routines (a plugin) | Recurring tasks and routines (Pro) |
| AI | None | Structured AI builds a day from text or a photo of a paper planner; Replan moves missed tasks (Pro) |
| Goals | Vision → goals → milestones; tasks link to them; a goal-flow diagram | Not a feature on its site |
| Deadlines | Live countdowns grouped by urgency, with a month calendar | Deadlines on tasks |
| Focus | Focus mode (key `F`) with the top three actions | Pomodoro focus timer; Intervals (Pro) |
| Daily review | Morning **Briefing** and evening **Debrief** ask only what needs a decision | Replan (Pro, iPhone) |
| Extras | Optional plugins, a simpler Minimalist console | Energy Monitor, widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch |

## Pick Structured if

- you plan mostly on your phone and want a native app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch or Android
- you want your Google, Outlook or Apple calendar on the same timeline as your tasks
- you like a bright, simple timeline more than a planning method
- you'd use AI to build or rebuild your day

## Pick Life Console if

- you don't want a subscription, or want your planner on your own server
- you plan the week, not just the day, and want to see when a day is overfull
- you work toward long-term goals and want each task to show which goal it serves
- you want deadlines as time left, counting down, and a short review each evening

## Moving a Structured routine to Life Console

![Plan week in Life Console: backlog, a 7-hour workload bucket per day and the day's timeline](/media/week@2x.webp)

1. **Inbox → Backlog.** Untimed tasks wait in the backlog. Quick-add takes one line per task, such as `Draft report 90m due:fri p2`.
2. **Timeline → Plan week.** Drag tasks into a day until its 7-hour bar is full, then onto the timeline, or press **Auto time-box** to fill free gaps by priority.
3. **Calendar events → fixed blocks.** There's no calendar import, so add the meetings that shape your day as blocks once a week.
4. **Replan → Debrief.** Each evening the Debrief takes every unfinished task one decision at a time: tomorrow, the backlog, or done.
5. **Long-term plans → Strategy.** Write goals and milestones, and tag tasks with a goal so the week shows what it moves forward.

There's no importer from Structured. Most people only need to bring over the tasks still ahead, and pasting them one line each is quick.
